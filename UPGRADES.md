# Kitty Kingdom: Upgrade Trees

Every building in Kitty Kingdom has four levels. Level 1 is the building itself. Level 2 is a choice of 3 branches, and level 3 is a choice of 3 finishers that depend on the branch. Level 4 is a choice of 2 capstones, which are the same for every route on that building. Choices are permanent for the run.

**Castle gates:**

- Level 2 needs Castle level 2, except for Houses, Farms, Walls and the Windmill.
- Level 3 needs Castle level 3.
- Level 4 needs Castle level 4.
- Ranged Halls, the Clinic, Scratching Post, Pounce Gym, Squad Den and Grand Library can only be built once the Castle is level 2.

In game, the nearest building's choices appear in a panel on the side of the screen opposite the building. Walk the King onto the matching numbered point (1, 2 or 3) and stand still to buy it. The panel only shows what you can buy right now.

## How this differs from Thronefall

- **Thronefall:** a tower's level-2 and level-3 picks are independent. Any spire can follow any specialization, and only a few buildings have choices at all.
- **Kitty Kingdom:** every pick commits you to a branch, and each branch has its own three finishers. Every building type works this way, including houses, walls, farms, docks and mines.

Once the Grand Library is fully upgraded it offers **Kingdom Research** every day. Each research gives all towers, cats, buildings and the King +6% damage and HP, starting at 25 gold and costing 15 more each time.

Ideas are borrowed from other tower-defense and strategy games:

- **Kingdom Rush:** the base tower turns into Archer, Mage or Artillery, then specializes, the way Kingdom Rush towers branch at level 4.
- **Bloons TD 6:** money, support and detection paths, such as farms, villages, camo detection and banks.
- **Orcs Must Die and Stronghold:** traps and walls.
- **Warcraft III TD and Plants vs Zombies:** auras, slows and economy plants.
- **Hades and Diablo:** the dash styles.

Each option below names the game that inspired it.
